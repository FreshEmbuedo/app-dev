# My Favorite Series and Movies  

## *Stranger Things*  
**Genre:** Sci-Fi, Mystery, Thriller  
> “Friends don’t lie.”  

- **Main Characters:**  
  - Eleven  
  - Mike Wheeler  
  - Dustin Henderson  
  - Lucas Sinclair  
  - Will Byers  

**Why I Like It:**  
The mix of 80s nostalgia, supernatural mysteries, and strong friendships makes *Stranger Things* unforgettable. The story keeps getting darker and more emotional with each season.  

---

## *Our Beloved Summer*  
**Genre:** Romance, Slice of Life, Comedy-Drama  

> *“I thought we’d forgotten everything, but some memories never fade.”*  

**Plot Summary:**  
Two former high school lovers are forced to reunite years later when a documentary they filmed together becomes popular again.  

**Highlights:**  
1. Beautiful cinematography and music  
2. Realistic portrayal of relationships  
3. Strong emotional storytelling  

**Favorite Scene:**  
When Choi Woong and Yeon-su finally express their true feelings under the stars. 🌌  

---

## *Summer Strike*  
**Genre:** Drama, Slice of Life  

> *“I’m not doing anything—and that’s okay.”*  

**Storyline:**  
After getting tired of city life, a young woman decides to leave everything behind and move to a small seaside town, where she learns to find peace and meaning in simplicity.  

**Reasons I Love It:**  
- Calm and healing atmosphere  
- Relatable message about rest and self-discovery  
- Inspiring characters and quiet beauty  

---

> *“Sometimes doing nothing helps you find everything.”*
